# Anti-Fraud Statement

**Project:** PHB Dashboard
**Maintainer:** VOIDBOT0

## 1. Common Crypto Scams

If anyone contacts you claiming to represent this project and asks for:

- Your seed phrase
- Your private key
- Payment to release an inheritance
- Payment to unlock funds
- Personal identity documents
- Access to your wallet

**They are a scammer. Block them immediately.**

## 2. What This Project Will Never Do

The PHB project will **never**:
- Ask for your seed phrase or private key
- Ask you to pay a fee to receive funds
- Promise guaranteed returns
- Claim you have a secret inheritance
- Claim you have royal status, tax exemption, or special legal standing
- Request your bank details
- Ask you to sign a document to "prove" your identity

## 3. Verification

All genuine project activity is verifiable on-chain. If a claim cannot be verified on Basescan or through the official GitHub repository, it is not legitimate.

## 4. Reporting

If you have been targeted:
- **UK:** Action Fraud — 0300 123 2020
- **UK:** Citizens Advice — 0808 223 1133
- **US:** FTC — reportfraud.ftc.gov

## 5. Official Channels Only

The only official channels for this project are:
- GitHub: github.com/jvoidial/spirit-guide-token
- Dashboard: jvoidial.github.io/spirit-guide-token

Any other channel claiming to be official is not authorized.
