# CLARITY Act Compliance Statement

**Project:** PHB Dashboard
**Maintainer:** VOIDBOT0
**Legislation:** Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (H.R. 3633 / CLARITY Act)

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the compliance posture of the PHB project with respect to the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (CLARITY Act) and related legislation.

## 2. Classification of the Interface

The PHB dashboard is a **read-only data interface**. It is not:
- A trading platform
- An exchange
- A broker-dealer
- A DeFi trading protocol
- A messaging system as defined under Section 302 of the CLARITY Act

The interface does not route orders, execute transactions, or take custody of user funds at any time.

## 3. Classification of the Protocol

The underlying on-chain component consists of **Uniswap V2 liquidity pools** on the Base network. Uniswap V2 is:
- Automated: rules are predetermined and non-discretionary
- Non-custodial: users retain control of their own assets
- Immutable: no admin functions, no pause, no upgrade key, no blacklist

Under the CLARITY Act's definition, these pools would satisfy the criteria for a **Decentralized Finance Technology Protocol (DFTP)** , if and when that provision takes effect.

## 4. DFTP Requirements

| Requirement | Status |
|-------------|--------|
| Automated rule or algorithm (Prong 1) | ✅ Satisfied — constant product formula |
| Non-custodial operation (Prong 2) | ✅ Satisfied — users keep keys |
| No unilateral control (Exclusion A) | ✅ Satisfied — no admin functions |
| No discretionary operation (Exclusion B) | ✅ Satisfied — immutable contracts |
| No restriction capability (Exclusion C) | ✅ Satisfied — no pause or blacklist |

## 5. Token Classification

The tokens displayed (PIDX, SGUIDE, VDOO, PENNIES) are digital commodities, not securities. There is no investment of money in a common enterprise with expectation of profit derived from the efforts of others.

## 6. No Declaration Under the CLARITY Act

This document makes no certification, declaration, or filing under the CLARITY Act. No such filing is required at this time. This document is informational only.

## 7. Good Faith

The maintainer operates in good faith and will comply with applicable law in any jurisdiction where the project operates.
